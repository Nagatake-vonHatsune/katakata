# Kata-Kata in class: a short guide for Sam

Teacher mode, memory cards and progress reports (17 Nov 2026). Everything runs on your own device: no accounts, nothing
uploaded, works offline once the app has been opened. The key card to print is [teacher-mode-keys.md](teacher-mode-keys.md).

## 1. Teacher mode on a TV or projector

**Turn it on:** ⚙️ Settings → 🎓 **Teacher mode**. The screen switches to big type and a wide layout, and a green
**🎓 先生モード ✕** badge sits at the top of every screen. Tap the badge once to turn teacher mode off.

**Connect the screen:**

- **Laptop:** HDMI to the projector or TV. Press **⛶ Full screen** (top right). A presenter clicker works: forward and back.
- **iPad:** HDMI adapter, or AirPlay screen mirroring to an Apple TV or AirPlay TV. Hold the iPad sideways. ⛶ Full screen works.
- **iPhone:** HDMI adapter or AirPlay. Hold the phone sideways (the app reminds you if you hold it upright). Safari on iPhone
  has no full screen for web pages: add Kata-Kata to the Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and open it from there.
- **Android phone:** HDMI (USB-C) or Cast / Smart View. Sideways.

The screen stays awake while teacher mode is on. Sound comes out of the device, so with HDMI or AirPlay it plays through the
TV. If a word does not play the first time on an iPhone or iPad, tap ▶ once: Apple only lets sound start from a tap.

**Run a class activity:** on Home, the **Class launcher** comes first.

1. Pick a tab: **Chapters**, **Jambatan**, **Cari Benda** or **Nombor & Masa**, then a chapter, scene, board or part.
2. Pick an activity: Words, Frames, Dialogue, ⚡ Words, ⚡ Frames, ⚡ Dialogue, Cari Benda, ⚡ Cari Benda, Laju! (Nombor &
   Masa: Explore or Round). The number is how many slides.
3. Press **▶ Start**.

Everything is open in teacher mode, whatever the device's own learner has unlocked. Nothing moves on by itself: you press
**Next** (or →, Space, PageDown, or the clicker). Next shows the answer first, then moves on. **Back** goes back a slide.
**H** hides or shows the English and Japanese, so the class can guess first; **R** reveals; **1 to 4** mark what the class
called out; **P** plays the Malay again (▶ and 🐢 on screen for normal and slow).

**A class run never changes anyone's data:** no reviews, stamps, 🔥, points, streaks, records or mistakes are saved. The
learner on the device is exactly as before when the run ends.

## 2. One-to-one lessons: the memory-card routine

A **memory card** (💾) is one file with a student's whole progress, like a game console's memory card. It works on the
student's phone and on your device alike.

**At the start of the lesson**

1. The student taps 📒 Records → 💾 **Save memory card** on their phone and sends you the file (AirDrop, LINE, email).
2. On your device (teacher mode on): Home → 🎓 Teacher menu → 👥 **Students** → **Plug in a memory card**.
   - A new student becomes a new slot.
   - A student you already have: you see both dates (your slot's last practice, the card's save date) and choose
     **Update** (the card is merged in) or **Keep both**. The slot is auto-saved first, so nothing is lost.
3. Tap **Switch** on their slot. The top bar now says **👤 Playing as …**: ordinary practice (Today's practice, Review,
   Class prep, mini-games) now counts for that student. Class runs from the launcher still count for no one.

**At the end of the lesson**

4. Students → 💾 **Save memory card** on their slot, and send the file back to the student. They load it on their phone
   (📒 Records → 💾 **Load memory card** → Merge). A card with another student's name can never be merged into theirs.
5. 📄 **Progress report** on their slot makes one HTML page: progress per chapter, weakest words, every mistake with what
   they answered, the right answer and the note, their questions, and next steps. It opens on a phone and prints on A4.
   It follows the app language (English or Japanese), always with the Malay.
6. Switch back to your own slot, or simply turn teacher mode off: that always returns to your own learner.

Students can also make their own progress report in 📒 Records → 📄 **Progress report**.

**Teacher Desk** (`/teacher/`, linked from the teacher menu): drop in any mix of student files: the old send-to-teacher
files, memory cards and progress reports. The class agenda now lists **Mistakes across the class**; click a student's name
for their own page (next steps, mistakes to review, weakest words, open questions).

## 3. What to try first in class

1. **A chapter dialogue as a scene** (Chapters → 4 → Dialogue): press Next line by line; have pairs read each line aloud
   before you play it; press H at the end to show all the English.
2. **⚡ Dialogue reply steps** (Chapters → 4 → ⚡ Dialogue): at each «What does Tanaka say?», the class calls out 1, 2 or 3,
   you press that number, then R. The usage notes («balut» in ⚡ 8, «kasihan» in ⚡ 17) appear in full under their line.
3. **⚡ Frames with decoys**: the class tells you the tile order (press the tile numbers), then R. One tile is a decoy.
4. **Words with translations hidden** (Chapters → n → Words): show the Malay, the class says the meaning, R reveals it
   with the card note.
5. **Cari Benda on the TV**: the class points at the object; you press its number, then R.
6. **Nombor & Masa → Masa → Explore**: drag the clock hands on screen and have the class say the time; then a Round.
7. **Laju!** with the 60-second clock on (switch on the launcher) for a team race at the end of the lesson.
